# Roadmap

The course is a path of themes. Go through them **in order, at your own pace**. Move on when the "ready" check of a theme works without help.

<div class="gh-only">

![The roadmap: three parts in order. Before loops: Hello World, Variables and expressions, Conditions. Loops: repeat and count, keep a running result, draw in 2D, take digits apart, remember changing values, search, combine recipes. After loops: Arrays and text, Files, Exceptions, Collections, Methods.](diagrams/roadmap.svg)

</div>

Before the first theme, read [How to read an exercise](docs/reading-an-exercise.md). You will use it for every exercise.

## After the roadmap

Objects and classes come next in most courses. There are no exercises for them here yet: follow your course or the book, and come back to the [cookbook](cookbook/README.md) when a method needs a loop.
