# Extra exercises: the C# Quest Tracker

The book has its own challenges at the end of each level. The [C# Quest Tracker](https://lfarci.github.io/csharp-quest-tracker/) is a free website to tick them, earn XP and level up, like a game.

## How it works

- Open the tracker in your browser. No account.
- Tick a challenge when you finish it. Your XP and level go up.
- Progress is saved in this browser. Use **Settings** to export a backup and import it on another computer.

## When to use it

Finish the exercises of a theme here first. Then, for more practice, do the book's challenges of the same level and track them there.
