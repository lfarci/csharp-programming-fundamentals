# The book: The C# Player's Guide

*The C# Player's Guide* (5th edition), by RB Whitaker, teaches C# from zero, in short **levels**. It is the theory companion of this course: we do not copy it, we point to the right level.

## How to use it with this course

1. Open the theme you are on.
2. Read the book level in its *Learn the theory* box.
3. Do the exercises of the theme here.
4. Want more? Do the book's own challenges and tick them in the [C# Quest Tracker](quest-tracker.md).
