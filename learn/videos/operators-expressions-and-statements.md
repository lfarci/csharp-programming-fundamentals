# Operators, Expressions, and Statements

*C# Fundamentals for Absolute Beginners*, on Microsoft Learn. 15 minutes.

<p><a class="cpf-button" href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/shows/csharp-fundamentals-for-absolute-beginners/operators-expressions-and-statements/">Watch on Microsoft Learn</a></p>

## Watch for

- The arithmetic operators, including `%` (remainder).
- Integer division: `7 / 2` is `3`.
- Short forms like `+=` and `++`.

## Key words

*Modulo (`%`)*, *Expression*. See the [glossary](../../docs/glossary.md).
